Use this answer when capable specialists produce locally satisfactory results, yet the project cannot obtain a usable whole, or when the organization must change how it obtains those contributions. **Start with the result someone needs to use; recover the work and relations that make it obtainable; then compare the arrangements and their consequences.** A useful first return can be the exact missing contribution and who can supply it. A sufficient specialist answer can already settle the question.

## Recover the needed contribution from the whole result {#recover-the-needed-contribution-from-the-whole-result}

In this constructed case, an instrument maker must demonstrate that two modules work together under an agreed acceptance condition. Two teams design the modules. A shared laboratory provides a rig and specialist, and the stipulated acceptance rule requires judgement independent of the design work. Both modules pass their local tests, but their fastening interfaces have incompatible dimensions. The local results therefore leave the intended integrated use unsupported.

For this offering project, the system to be brought about is the integrated instrument. Its intended use, configuration and acceptance question determine what each contribution must establish. [SYSE.1](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-syse/contract/SYSE-1#root) helps recover that project-relative subject when it is unclear. The laboratory is a provider to this project; its continuing service has its own recipients and commitments.

Ask the engineers to recover the common interface conditions and which design choices were fixed or left open. Obtain the missing mechanical judgement about a compatible fastening arrangement. [SYSE.9](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-syse/contract/SYSE-9#root) makes the request precise: which configuration and use it concerns, what result is needed, and what the engineering decision will do with it. Qualified mechanical practice supplies the fastening and test Methods. [SYSE.6](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-syse/contract/SYSE-6#root) connects the choice to the whole instrument: compare the consequences, decide the shared constraint under the relevant authority, and leave unaffected module details with their contributors. Suppose the qualified engineering comparison supports a common mounting pattern. The decision selects that interface and the integrated test needed to judge it; both teams update to the identified configuration C7.

General engineering reasoning makes the whole, its use, interfaces and evidence dependencies explicit. It helps find the professional work still needed. The particular calculation, physical test and interpretation need their own Methods and capable performers. “Mechanical engineer” is useful for finding professional knowledge and possible contributors; the present contribution is the justified interface answer for this configuration. A role name alone establishes neither that answer nor the person's capability to produce it.

Follow the result through its receiving use:

| Contribution in this case | What another participant needs from it |
| --- | --- |
| Module design and integration | A compatible configuration and the identified conditions under which the modules must work together. |
| Laboratory service | A feasible test arrangement with the required rig, specialist, inputs and usable interval; subsequently, the performed test and its returned evidence. |
| Evidence integration | An accessible package identifying the tested configuration, relevant results, limits and unresolved claims. |
| Independent acceptance judgement | A supported acceptance or reasoned return under the stipulated criteria and authority. A return identifies the claim or condition that prevents acceptance. |

Follow the relations within the work as well as its timing. While a specialist interprets a trace, that interpretation can contribute to judging an interface claim within preparation of the whole acceptance package. The integrated-use question determines what the trace must establish. Meanwhile, booking the laboratory enables the test without itself performing the test or the acceptance judgement. A test description, the ability to use it, actual test Work and the resulting report supply different parts of this account.

## Resolve the conflict at the relation that failed {#resolve-the-conflict-at-the-relation-that-failed}

One person may design and integrate if the combined work fits their capability, assignment and available time. Under this case's independent-acceptance rule, that designer cannot also supply the required independent judgement. A senior technician proposed as a replacement test performer must fit the actual test and its admission conditions; seniority alone supplies no substitution basis. FPF [E.10.ROLE](spec://ai.lev/fpf-spec/contract/E-10-ROLE#root) and [A.2.7](spec://ai.lev/fpf-spec/contract/A-2-7#root) help unpack “combined role”: which assignments may share a holder, which require different holders, and how the Methods or work combine. Use these relations instead of treating role labels as parts of the person or project.

Now suppose the returned report concerns configuration C6, preceding the interface repair. The receiver needs applicable evidence for the C7 claim. Another reviewer appointment cannot make that report applicable. Preserve any results whose conditions still fit, and obtain only the missing evidence or justified applicability account.

Participants can also disagree for different reasons. The department head wants an earlier date, a designer objects that the proposed interface violates a required tolerance, and the laboratory manager reports that the needed interval is already committed. Recover the grounds of each statement. The tolerance needs an engineering answer; allocation of laboratory time needs the provider's decision and a feasible service plan; a proposed change of date needs agreement by the parties whose commitment changes. Ask each receiver what the proposed repair lets them do and what it leaves impossible.

Professional concerns help discover relevant characteristics, but inspect the actual concern. A low quoted test price can coexist with higher total cost after preparation, transport, support and displaced work. Specify which cost and period the comparison uses. Keep a protected technical condition distinct from a preference that the authorized parties can trade. [OCE.12](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-12#root) develops the explanation, challenge and negotiation needed to obtain participation in organizational change, including the changes to assignments and support that discussion alone cannot supply.

## Carry the reasoning into a project that changes the organization {#carry-the-reasoning-into-a-project-that-changes-the-organization}

Suppose the instrument maker repeatedly relies on the same manager to reconstruct missing inputs, find a laboratory interval and obtain an acceptance return. Management now wants the engineering organization to obtain that complete contribution with less exceptional intervention. This is a second project. Its changed system is the engineering organization; its result is a workable arrangement for obtaining contributions across the module teams, laboratory and acceptance work. The instrument remains the subject of the offering project. The integrator's ability to interpret a trace is a further question for human capability development.

Recover the organization's intended contribution through [OCE.1](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-1#root), then use [OCE.2](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-2#root) to examine how representative work actually reaches its receiver, including informal help. A report may be complete while the next participant is still waiting: they may lack its location, version identity or notice that it is ready. Faster testing would leave that delivery difficulty unresolved. Recover the request, promise, production, recognizable return, acceptance and correction path together.

The general questions transfer: what whole must work, what result is needed, which relations make it obtainable, and which consequence would defeat the arrangement? Their domain answers change. A fastening calculation addresses the instrument. Contribution design, position establishment and effective appointments address the organization. Similar titles such as “architect” in the two projects do not establish corresponding Methods. [C.32.MWA](spec://ai.lev/fpf-spec/contract/C-32-MWA#root) helps compare ways to organize Method, Work, capability and provider relations, including the burden an arrangement moves to other work. Use the OCE Methods for the organization decisions.

Apply [OCE.7](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-7#root) when the instrument and organization choices constrain each other. Compare changing the organization, changing the product, changing both, and retaining their different boundaries with explicit coordination. In this case, suppose merging the groups would disrupt a specialist-service commitment, while separating the shared test requires a redesign that cannot fit the next two releases. Retaining the module teams with one integration contribution and an agreed laboratory service is then a serious candidate. Its cost is shared-test coordination, and each group's release depends on the joint result.

The engineering and organization decision-makers each decide within their authority. The product decision retains the module and test boundaries; the organization decision selects how the integration contribution and exception return will work. Reopen the pair if the shared test burden defeats the protected service commitment, or when the two-release horizon ends. The laboratory provider still has to make the resource decision on which this choice depends. If it cannot, the preferred arrangement remains unsupported.

## Establish and exercise the arrangement {#establish-and-exercise-the-arrangement}

Use [OCE.4](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-4#root) to design the contribution path for the selected arrangement. Specify who supplies the identified package, who uses it, what makes it usable, how an unsupported claim returns for correction, and which timing and support conditions must hold. Keep separate specialist, evidence and acceptance decisions where the work requires them. If a continuing position must remain identifiable through vacancy or holder replacement, [OCE.5](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-5#root) supplies its institutional basis and expected contribution. A direct assignment can suffice when no such position identity matters.

Then use [OCE.6](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-6#root) to establish the actual appointment and the separately needed authority, access and provision. In the case, the evidence integrator's appointment can be effective while repository access is still pending. The appointment remains effective, but package preparation cannot rely on unavailable access. Naming someone responsible does not allocate the laboratory, supply specialist capability or change the acceptance rule.

Suppose the integrator can assemble the package but cannot interpret a newly required trace. Compare a missing skill with a misleading description, unsuitable test, missing access or incompatible workload through [HCD.3](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-hcd/contract/HCD-3#root). Use compatible existing observations; obtain a distinguishing observation only when needed. For a supported learning need, give a qualified learning provider the trace-interpretation task, required grounds and later conditions of use. Obtain practice and feedback against those criteria, with the time, tools and later opportunity required. A capable specialist can instead supply the interpretation when that arrangement meets the receiving conditions. A checklist or reminder permitted in the work can remain part of supported performance. The change organizer also needs the expertise to distinguish these causes; obtain qualified engineering or learning help when that judgement is beyond their capability.

Use [OCE.11](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-11#root) to fit the complete change into continuing work. Package preparation, practice, specialist feedback, test setup, ordinary service and recovery can compete for the same people and interval. A learning proposal without released time leaves the contribution unavailable. [OPS.13](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-ops/contract/OPS-13#root) connects the service promise to that complete work and resource basis, including acceptance. Keep any old obligation visible until the authorized parties accept a revision.

Use [OCE.9](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-oce/contract/OCE-9#root) to exercise a small complete use under the selected design and trial conditions. A team requests the test; the provider confirms feasible conditions; the test produces evidence; the integrator returns the identified package; the independent contributor accepts it or gives a reasoned correction. Exercise an unusable return as well as an ordinary success. If the report exists but its receiver cannot find the current version, repair that crossing and repeat it within the whole contribution.

Observe later work under the conditions the organization needs to rely on. If reduced dependence on the initiating manager matters, include an episode without that manager reconstructing the path. Retain any allowed specialist help. A successful facilitated attempt supports what happened with that help; it does not establish the less dependent capability. The operating owner accepts the continuing service and support consequences. Completing the organization project does not discharge outstanding instrument or laboratory commitments.

## Reconsider the affected choice when conditions change {#reconsider-the-affected-choice-when-conditions-change}

Withdraw the laboratory interval. The prior service plan no longer supplies the intended test in that period. Return the consequence to the provider and service owner while an alternative interval, provider, test arrangement or agreed date remains possible. Carry any changed engineering conditions back to the engineer: a different test can change what its evidence supports. Preserve unaffected module work and compatible evidence.

Withdraw acceptance authority instead. Obtain the required authority decision or another eligible, capable and authorized contributor under the independence rule. Extra laboratory hours leave that gap open. If authority remains but later work requires recognizing an unfamiliar trace problem without a prompt, return the changed demand and observations to HCD.3. Where an ordinary reminder is allowed and sufficient, preserve that supported arrangement; require self-initiated recognition only when the work needs it.

A repair can expose another conflict. Return to the lost-interval case, with the other required contributions still available. Suppose an alternative provider offers a night test with the required rig and specialist. The proposed interval fits the test work, while a preliminary site account indicates possible disturbance to a nearby resident. That person performs no project work and cannot attend the meeting. Their possible loss still belongs in the choice. [SYSE.17](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-syse/contract/SYSE-17#root) helps trace the exposure, state what is known and uncertain, and return the consequence to the decision that can alter it. The preliminary account is no measurement of the proposed test.

Compare an available daytime interval or quieter arrangement, using [SYSE.2](spec://ai.lev/fpf-eng-syse/contract/SYSE-2#root) if the new consequence changes the linked use or system concept. Obtain a bounded acoustic or other specialist result when it can change the decision and warrants its full burden. Permission and any needed representation of the resident require their own basis. A technically successful test would settle neither the disturbance question nor the relevant permission. Feed the resulting constraint or uncertainty into the laboratory and organization choice, retaining the valid engineering work.

Finish this inquiry when the next useful result, its receiving use and obtainable contribution are clear enough to act, or when the exact missing condition and return are known. Ordinary coordination can suffice without an organization-change project.
